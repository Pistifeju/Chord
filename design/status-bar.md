# Status bar

A 26px strip across the window's full width, under the sidebar and the main area, on `canvas` with a 1px `line` top edge. Its items sit on the strip's center line. The 28px gear icon button that opens Settings (tooltip "Settings (⌘,)") sits in a 36px footer row at the sidebar's bottom, 8px in, under a 1px `line` divider. At the row's right end, 8px in, a question mark icon button (tooltip "Help") opens the Help sheet (see Sheets). Both hide with the sidebar; ⌘, and the app menu still open Settings. The strip's items sit at the right with 12px padding, in 11px `ink-muted`, tabular figures. The strip always shows. Subscription usage shows one group per harness that reported it in this run, in the harness list's order (pi, Codex, Claude Code), 16px apart; a group starts with the harness's 12px logo, 8px before its bar, and reads `42% 2h 48m  ·  59% 4d 6h`: each window's percentage and the time left to its reset in its two largest units, windows joined by a middle dot with two spaces each side, refreshed each minute. Before it, 8px away, a 32 × 4px bar (2px radius, `line-strong` track, `accent-ink` fill; above 70% `status-waiting`, above 85% `status-error`) shows the shortest window's percentage. A group's tooltip names the harness, then lists each window as `5h 42% (resets 14:30)`; a reset a day or more away carries its weekday. A window past its reset is hidden, and a group with no window left goes. A group is a button: a click opens the Usage panel (see `design/usage-panel.md`), and hover turns its text to `ink` over 120ms. A login without a subscription (an API key) reports no window, so its harness shows no group. In a worktree with an Xcode project the simulators button sits before the usage (see Build and Run).
