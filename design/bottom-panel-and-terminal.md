# Bottom panel and Terminal

- **Bottom panel:** one row under the panes and the editor, left of the side panel, 240px tall by default, on `canvas` with a 1px `line` top edge. Dragging the top edge resizes it between 90px and 800px, and it leaves the panes 160px at least; the panel stops at 90px, and dragging the pointer 45px past that snaps it shut; dragging back up snaps it open at 90px, and letting go while it is shut hides it with every view in it; the edge has a divider's 9px grab area centered on the border and takes `line-strong` on hover and drag. Open state and height are saved per worktree. It shows the Terminal, and in a worktree with an Xcode project the Build log or the Console in a second column right of it (see Build and Run).
- **Terminal:** ⌘T shows or hides the worktree's terminal in the bottom panel and focuses it when it opens. One login shell per worktree starts on its first show and keeps running, with its scrollback, while the panel is hidden or another worktree shows; typing `exit` or deleting the worktree closes the panel, and the next ⌘T starts a new shell. A 28px header with a 1px `line` bottom rule: "Terminal" at 12px/500 in `ink-secondary`, `ink` while the terminal has the focus, and at the right end a 24px Hide icon button (tooltip "Show or Hide Terminal (⌘T)"), 4px from the edge. Under it xterm.js on `canvas` with 8px top, 2px bottom and 8px left padding, its rows held to the bottom edge, or to the top edge while the screen has no scrollback and the cursor has not reached the last row: `ink` text and block cursor, `editor-selection` selection, the 16 ANSI colors from the `term-*` tokens (xterm's Tango palette in dark; GitHub's light terminal palette in light, so yellow and white stay readable on white), `ui-monospace` (SF Mono) at the terminal font size, 10,000 lines of scrollback (Settings › Appearance › Terminal, 9 to 24px, 12px by default). ⌘ keys run the app's shortcuts and copy and paste; every other key goes to the shell.
- **Terminal links:** a web URL in the terminal output opens in the browser with ⌘-click; a plain click does nothing.
- **Terminal find:** ⌘F while the terminal has the focus opens the chat's find bar (Chat › Find) under the header, at the terminal's top right. Matches take `editor-match` laid over `canvas` (xterm takes only opaque colors); the current one adds a 1px `accent-ink` border and scrolls into view. Esc closes the bar and returns the focus to the terminal.
