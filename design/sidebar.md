# Sidebar

The sidebar uses the app's roles; `src/ui/tokens.css` overrides `selected` inside `.sidebar`: white in light, white at 6% in dark, and adds `card`, the fill of a resting worktree card: `raised` in light, white at 3% in dark.

- 280px, full window height, `panel` fill with a 1px `line` right edge.
- **Navigator:** under the title bar row, a Segmented control across the sidebar, 8px in, 4px above the view: one icon segment per view, each a third of the track with its 16px icon centered in `ink-muted` (`ink` on hover and when chosen), pressing it scales the icon to 90%, tooltip with its shortcut, as in Xcode's navigator. Workspaces (two stacked cards, ⌃1), Files (a folder, ⌃2, and ⌘⇧E), and Changes (the branch, ⌃3, and ⌘⇧G). The sidebar shows the chosen view between the control and the footer; without an open worktree only Workspaces shows. ⌘⇧E and ⌘⇧G go back to Workspaces while their view shows, and any of the shortcuts shows a hidden sidebar. While any agent awaits input with a dialog open or a question you have not opened, the Workspaces segment carries a 6px `status-waiting` dot at its icon's top right with a 1.5px `raised` ring, gone while Workspaces shows, so a card the sidebar hides still calls. The choice lasts until the app quits.
- **Files and Changes in the sidebar:** the Files view and the Changes view (see Side panel and Files, Changes view) as they were in the side panel, on the sidebar's `panel` fill; the Changes view's sticky headings take `panel` too.
- **Workspaces:** the header is a 32px row, 4px above the list: the "Workspaces" title (Label weight in `ink`) 18px in, on the project names' edge, and the 28px add icon button with its 16px plus in `ink-muted` (`ink` on hover), its glyph on the shortcut hints' right edge.
- **List:** one group per project, in project order, scrolling inside.
  The groups sit 16px apart with no rule between them: the filled cards part the projects.
  A group is the project name (11px/600 `ink-muted`, uppercase, 0.06em letter spacing, 16px line, 6px above and below, 10px left padding so it aligns with the card text, truncates; right-click opens New Worktree and Remove Project), then its worktree cards 4px apart.
  No folding.
- **Shortcuts:** ⌘1 to ⌘9 follow the cards top to bottom.
- **Worktree card:** one Inset-radius card holds the worktree's head and its session rows. Only the head and the rows take clicks. The head has 8px 10px padding, 2px between its lines, `ink-muted` meta text, one or two lines:
  - **Title line** (20px): the branch at 13px/500 `ink`, which truncates, and the shortcut hint at 11px `ink-muted` at the right end (`ink-secondary` on hover and when open); the line centers its items, and the hint never shrinks.
  - **Meta line** (16px, 12px meta), only when there are counts or a run marker: the git counts at 11px/500 tabular ("↑2 ↓1" in the meta color, then the changed files as "~33" in `git-changed`), which never shrink, then the worktree's run marker (see Build and Run).
- **Session rows:** inside the card under the head, 4px bottom padding after the last, one 26px row per chat tab of the worktree, in layout order, 10px side padding so the logo starts on the branch's edge, Inset radius, 8px between its logo, its name and its status slot.
  The logo is the 16px logo of the pane's harness, the chat icon in `ink-muted` until the pane's first start, with the harness's name as its tooltip.
  The name is the tab's name (see Tab bar and panes) at 13px `ink-muted`, which truncates. A click opens the worktree and that tab in its group, with the focus. Hover only brightens the name to `ink` over 120ms, with no fill; the open worktree's focused tab takes `ink` at 500. The status slot sits at the row's right end, on every worktree's rows, open or not. It never shrinks, 11px/500 in the status color, the tooltip holds the pane's last message: the orbit and the run's age ("4m", none in the first minute) while its agent runs, an 8px dot and "Awaiting input", "Failed" or the exit; a finished session nobody opened shows a dot and "Done" in `status-done`. An agent's question counts as seen once you open the session; an open dialog keeps "Awaiting input" until you answer it. A session that is done or asked and seen shows the time since that status began ("now", "5m", "3h", "2d") in 11px `ink-muted`, tabular.
- **Attention dock:** over the footer row, in every navigator view, a column with a 1px `line` top edge, 6px 8px padding, 2px between its parts, 40% of the sidebar's height at most, scrolling inside. A Working row while any agent runs, a Needs you row while the attention queue (Next Agent Needing You, ⌘J) holds agents besides the card's, then one card. A row is 28px, 8px left and 6px right padding, so its label lines up with the footer's Settings gear and its chevron with the Help mark, Inset radius, 12px/500 `ink-muted`: its label, its count at 11px tabular at the right, then the 120ms turn chevron; hover lays `card` and takes `ink-secondary`. A click unfolds the row's agents under it as session rows (see Session rows), one row open at a time. The card is the agent ⌘J opens next, else the queue's first: `raised`, Inset radius, 8px 10px padding, 2px between three lines: the project name as the list's project names with the status mark, word and age ("Awaiting input · 12m") at its right in the status color; the session name at 13px/500 `ink`; the branch at 12px `ink-muted` with the ⌘J hint at the right as a card's shortcut hint, only while ⌘J opens it. The focused pane's card takes a 1px `line-strong` inset ring. A new card fades in and rises 4px over 200ms (fades only under Reduce Motion); pressing it scales it to 98.5%. With no agent running or needing you the dock hides.
- **Reorder:** dragging a project's name moves the project with its cards; dragging a card's head moves the card within its project, never into another. A press becomes a drag after 5px, so a click still selects. The dragged group or card dims to 60%, the tab drag's chip with its name follows the pointer, and a 2px `accent-ink` line with a 1px radius marks where it lands: between two cards, or in the space between two groups. Dropping right before or after itself shows no line and changes nothing. Near the list's top or bottom edge the list scrolls. The main checkout's card moves like any other, and ⌘1 to ⌘9 follow the new order. A new worktree goes to the end of its project.
- **Card menu:** right-click opens New Worktree (for its project), Rename and Delete Worktree (neither on the main checkout) and Remove Project.
- **More button:** a 20px icon button (three dots, Inset radius, `ink-muted`, tooltip "More") at the right end of a project's name and of a card's title line, 6px from the edge.
  It shows while the pointer or the focus is on the project's name or on the card, fades in and out over 120ms, and takes no room when hidden.
  On a card the title line's right end moves in 22px over the same 120ms, so the shortcut hint makes room for it.
  Hover brings its glyph to `ink` with no fill.
  A click opens the menu that right-click opens there.
  A card whose branch is being renamed shows none.
- **Branch name field:** Rename, or a double click on the branch name, turns the head into a 20px field (`canvas`, 1px `line-strong` border, Control radius) with the branch at 13px/500 selected, its text on the branch's edge. Git's reason for a name it refuses sits under it as a field hint in `danger`. Return renames the branch, Esc or leaving the field drops it.
- **States:** a card rests on `card`.
  Hover lays a 1px `line` inset ring on the whole card over 120ms.
  Pressing the head or a session row scales the card to 98.5% over 120ms.
  The head and the rows take no fill of their own.
  Hover on the head brings the shortcut hint to `ink-secondary`.
  The open worktree's whole card, head and rows, takes the sidebar's `selected`, a 1px `line-strong` inset ring and `ink-secondary` meta text.
  The focus ring sits inside the head or the row, so it clears its neighbors.
