# Toast

One surface in the title bar row, 32px tall at least, 560px wide at most.
Its text is selectable; a 24px close icon button (tooltip "Dismiss") sits 4px from its right end.
A notice shows text only and closes after 4 seconds.
A failure shows a `danger` warning icon before the text and stays until its close button, so an install step can be read and copied.
